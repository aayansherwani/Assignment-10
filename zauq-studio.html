How We Work

Four steps, one thread

A collaborative sequence from first conversation to final reveal — nothing skipped, nothing rushed at the finish.

i.

Consult

We walk the site, hear how you actually live or work, and set the brief before a single sketch happens.

ii.

Design

Layouts, material boards and 3D views, built around what's genuinely sourceable here — no imported placeholders.

iii.

Refine

Two structured review rounds where we adjust for budget, light and how the space will actually be used.

iv.

Execute

On-site supervision through to the last coat and the last cushion, coordinated with your contractor directly.
